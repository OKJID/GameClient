Generals Online

Play C&C Generals Zero Hour on Mac

macOS Gatekeeper will block this app because it is a free community port without a paid Apple Developer certificate. You need to remove the quarantine flag using Terminal before playing.
Telegram Channel Support Developer Check for Updates

Open Terminal

Open the Terminal application from Launchpad or Spotlight Search.

Type the command

Copy the following command. Paste it into the Terminal (it has a necessary space at the end), but do not press Enter yet:

sudo xattr -cr

Drag and Drop

Drag the unzipped Generals Online.app into the Terminal window. Its file path will be inserted automatically. Then press Enter.

Enter your Mac Password

The Terminal will ask for your Mac administrator password (the PIN or password you use to unlock your computer).

⚠️ Wait! Your typing will be invisible!

When you type your password, no characters or asterisks (*) will appear on the screen. This is a normal macOS security feature. Just type your password blindly and press Enter.

Open the Launcher

You can now double-click the app to open the Launcher! Choose how you want to get the Windows game data — download it from Steam or point the launcher at a copy you already have.
